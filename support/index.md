# Rukarta Support

Rukarta is a Russian verb and aspect learning app. If something is not working, or you have a question or a suggestion, this page should help.

- the verb you were looking at, if it is about a specific verb or sentence.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is free, and what is Rukarta Pro?

Levels A1 and A2 are free. Rukarta Pro unlocks the B1 and B2 verbs and the rest of the app's content.

### How do I restore my purchase?

Open any locked verb or level to reach the Pro screen, then tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Rukarta Pro is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple. To manage or cancel it, open the **Settings** app, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, and choose Rukarta. The subscription renews unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

### I was charged but Pro is not unlocked.

Tap **Restore Purchases** on the Pro screen first. If that does not help, email me with the date of the purchase and your Apple receipt, and I will look into it. Refund requests are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Can I use the app without an internet connection?

Yes. Verbs, forms and most example sentences are stored in the app. A few verbs may look up extra example sentences online, so those need a connection.

### There is a mistake in a definition, translation or example sentence.

Please email me the verb and the sentence. Example sentences come from open example-sentence services and from original teaching examples written for the app, and some of them may contain errors or sound unnatural. Corrections are welcome.

### How do I turn the sound or read-aloud on or off?

Tap the speaker icon on a sentence to turn read-aloud on or off. Read-aloud uses the Russian voice installed on your device. For better quality, open **Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > Russian** and download an Enhanced voice. The wheel tick sound follows your device volume.

### How do I use the wheels and the card?

- Turn the category, level, stem, prefix and ending wheels to build or change a verb.
- Swipe the card sideways to flip it to the aspect partner.
- Swipe the sentence sideways to see another example sentence.

### Where is my study history stored?

Only on your device. The app has no accounts and sends no study data anywhere. Deleting the app removes it. See the [Privacy Policy](https://jasonnaya001-boop.github.io/rukarta-legal/).
