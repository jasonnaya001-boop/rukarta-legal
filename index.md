# Rukarta Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 11, 2026
**Developer:** Jason Naya
**Contact:** jasonnaya@live.com

Rukarta ("the app", "we") is a Russian verb learning app. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does not. In short: Rukarta has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and we do not collect personal information about you.

## Information we do not collect

- We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal details.
- We do not use the microphone or the camera.
- We do not use advertising or analytics tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
- We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it.

## Information stored on your device

To work as a study tool, the app saves a small amount of data **only on your device**, such as which verbs you have opened and how often, and your practice history. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It stays on your device until you delete the app or clear its data, at which point it is removed.

## Internet requests

The app mostly works with content stored inside it. For some verbs it may look up extra example sentences from online example-sentence services over the internet. When it does, the request includes the verb being looked up. As with any internet request, the service receiving it can see your device's IP address and basic technical information such as your device type and app version. We do not control those services and they have their own privacy practices. No account or personal information from you is included in these requests.

## Read-aloud feature

If you turn on read-aloud, the app uses the speech voices built into your device's operating system to speak example sentences. Whether your device processes speech on the device or online depends on your device and its settings, and is governed by the operating system provider's privacy policy (Apple or Google). The app does not record or store anything you hear.

## Purchases

The app offers a free tier and may offer paid access to additional content ("Pro"). If you buy it, the purchase is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment card details. We may receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock the content.

## Children

Rukarta is a general-audience educational app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Data security

Because your study data stays on your device, its protection depends on your device's own security, such as a passcode and device encryption.

## Your choices

You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your device settings. You can control read-aloud and sound through your device settings.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing or in the app.
